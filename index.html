BDET-mobility — Architecture & Data Pipeline

Authors: Julia García & Carlos Torregrosa

A high-performance Data Lakehouse implementation designed to analyze human mobility patterns across Spain during 2023 by correlating massive MITMA datasets with INE socio-economic indicators.

Medallion Architecture:
  • Bronze Layer (Raw Ingestion): Automated extraction of billions of trip records at District, Municipality, and GAU levels from S3, ingesting structured CSVs and spatial GeoJSONs via DuckDB ST_Read.
  • Silver Layer (Harmonization & Spatial Engineering): Schema enforcement, MITMA-to-INE zonification code resolution, spatial joins with calculated centroids, and a multi-billion row integrated fact table.
  • Gold Layer (Advanced Analytics): Unsupervised K-Means clustering on hourly mobility vectors, Gravity Model infrastructure gap identification, and workday-to-weekend industrial hub detection.
Technical Stack:
  • Core Engine: DuckDB (vectorized SQL OLAP)
  • Orchestration: Apache Airflow with Dynamic Task Mapping
  • Storage & Metadata: Amazon S3 Data Lake + Neon (DuckLake metadata)
  • Visualization & ML: KeplerGL, Scikit-Learn, FPDF

LaLiga Monte Carlo Predictor — Methodology

Author: Carlos Torregrosa Alcayde (Project: Heuristics 2025)

Applies Data Science, Discrete-Event Simulation (DES), and Monte Carlo methods to predict final outcomes of the LaLiga season by simulating remaining matches thousands of times in parallel.

Simulation Workflow:
  • Data Initialization: Standings state initialization with weighted win/draw/loss probability distributions based on team form.
  • Discrete-Event Simulation (SimPy): Time-based progression simulating matchday-by-matchday until Matchday 38 with stochastic resolution.
  • Monte Carlo Risk Aggregation: 2,000+ simulation runs generating rank distributions, relegation safety gaps, and best/worst case bounds.
Technologies Used:
  • Python 3.9+, SimPy (DES), Streamlit, Matplotlib & Pandas

Murdoku — Detective Puzzle Engine

Un rompecabezas lógico interactivo que fusiona la colocación de fichas típica de Sudoku con mecánicas de deducción al estilo Cluedo / Murder Mystery.

Características y Mecánicas:
  • Generación Procedimental Infinita: Acertijos generados en tiempo real mediante un resolvedor por backtracking ultrarrápido con solución lógica única garantizada.
  • Niveles de Dificultad: Fácil (5x5), Medio (6x6), Difícil (7x7).
  • Habitaciones Irregulares: División de zonas mediante algoritmo de crecimiento BFS aleatorio.
  • Herramientas de Investigación: Modo lápiz para notas, historial de pistas tachables, y resaltado interactivo de filas/columnas.
  • 100% Serverless: Ejecución nativa en el navegador del cliente.
Tecnologías:
  • JavaScript nativo, Algoritmos de Backtracking, Grafos BFS, HTML5 Canvas & CSS3
